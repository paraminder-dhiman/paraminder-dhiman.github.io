Video editing

DaVinci Resolve · Fusion · 5+ years

    I've been editing video in DaVinci Resolve for more than five years, mostly for my own gaming channel. The video below shows a year of that progress in one place.

    Gaming edits

    Highlights
    A multiplayer highlights video is mostly cutting. I pick the clips, trim them and fit the whole edit to a set length, then sync it to the music so every kill or big moment lands on a beat.
    Fusion
    I use Fusion for effects, such as a node setup for motion blur.
    Jigsaw transition
    On some edits I built a transition in Fusion where the next scene assembles on screen piece by piece, like a jigsaw puzzle.
    Grading
    Graded to make the footage brighter and more vibrant.
    Walkthroughs
    Full 100% walkthroughs are simpler work. It's about getting the clips in the right order, with light grading for vibrancy.

    Freelance and other edits

    I've also edited on a freelance basis and on projects for friends. That work was about continuity: arranging clips so a scene reads as one piece, and lining dubbed voice lines up with the lip movement on screen.

    Grading had a different job there. I used it to set the mood of a scene, happy or sad, instead of making it pop.
