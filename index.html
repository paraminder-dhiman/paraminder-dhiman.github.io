    Ornament placement tool
    Ornaments are vanity pieces placed around a ship. They used to be placed by hand in Houdini, which was slow and heavy for the job. I wrote a placement tool in 3ds Max with MaxScript that brought the work down from up to 1.5 hours to about 15 minutes per ship. It has been used on more than 22 ships, by art teams in our studio and overseas.
    Mirroring without negative scale
    The engine doesn't handle negative scale well, so a mirrored ornament can't simply be flipped. I worked out a quaternion rotation that gives a correct mirror on the other side of the hull. Getting it right took some time, and it's what made the placement tool usable.
    Sail animation pipeline
    Sails are simulated as cloth and baked down to bones with SSDR in Houdini. I took this pipeline over when it was broken, spent weeks working out how it functions, and now own it on the project.
    Ship delivery
    Supported the creation of new ships from concept art to the final working version in the engine.
    Houdini workflows
    New ships are built in Houdini. I optimised parts of that workflow so artists can iterate faster.
    Performance
